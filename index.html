How it works

  1. 1

    Log on your iPhone

    Lifting, runs and climbs, set by set. Your routine’s plan sits beside each set, and one tap logs it, signal or not.

  2. 2

    Connect your AI

    Add Cartwheel as a connector in Claude or ChatGPT, or any AI agent that speaks MCP. You approve it once, with your Cartwheel account.

  3. 3

    Your coach remembers

    Ask about your training and it answers from your log: every session, plus the goals and constraints you’ve written down.

Then ask your AI.

One log, read by whichever AI you use. Switch assistants and your history comes with you.
